SPEADE PDF Accessibility

How it works:

1. Click Add PDFs… to pick documents (or drop them in the input folder shown above).

2. Click Process PDFs, the app recognises text in scans and adds accessibility tags.

3. Pick a document from the list, check it, and record your decision.

Title and reading language, screen readers announce both. Check they are right (the title is what a student hears, not the filename) and save before you decide.
Your decision, the app only makes a draft. Check the document (fix anything needed in Adobe Acrobat first), then decide. Approving is the final step.
